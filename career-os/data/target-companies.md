## Company Criteria

Use these criteria when evaluating whether to add a company to the list.

**Include if:**
- Mission or product aligns with your background
- Company size and stage match your experience
- Culture signals are positive (Glassdoor ≥ 3.8, no layoff pattern)
- Leadership you respect
- Compensation is likely to meet your minimum

**Exclude if:**
- Known toxic culture (Glassdoor ≤ 3.5 with consistent leadership complaints)
- Recent mass layoffs (last 6 months) without clear recovery signal
- Company is contracting, not growing
- Compensation is publicly known to be below your minimum
